Game CG cutscene cinematic, photorealistic film-quality, epic dark fantasy. One continuous shot.

@[รูปภาพ4] — Starting frame.

mountainside. Timber-frame houses climbing the slope, dense stone rooftops,
warm window lights scattered like stars.
Dragon continues driving toward the city, mist ribbons torn from wingtips by each stroke.

Shallow DOF locked on dragon. Volumetric light through clouds.
ARRI tone, dark teal shadows, warm orange sky, film grain.

No background music. Only diegetic sounds — howling wind,
powerful wing beats hammering air, vapor explosion entering clouds, distant city ambience.
