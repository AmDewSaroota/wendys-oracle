## Lessons Learned

- When drafting business negotiation emails, ask about budget/pricing expectations BEFORE writing — saves iteration cycles
- "Academic pricing" is a stronger position than "free please" — shows you're a real customer with real budget
- Scale-up potential (12 → 300) is the strongest leverage in vendor negotiations — vendors invest in growth pipelines
- Published research acknowledgment is valuable currency for tech companies wanting academic credibility
