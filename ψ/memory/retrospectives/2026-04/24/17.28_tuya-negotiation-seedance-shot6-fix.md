## Honest Feedback

**Friction 1: Over-engineering prompts.** When DewS showed the Shot 6 screenshot, I wrote a completely new prompt from scratch with full action choreography. She had to tell me the action was fine — just add 2 things. I should ask "what specifically needs changing?" before rewriting everything. Less is more.

**Friction 2: Still no file artifacts.** Three sessions of email drafting and prompt writing with nothing saved. The Tuya negotiation thread is important business correspondence that should be archived somewhere. The Shot 6 prompt evolution is creative work that could inform future shots. All of it lives only in chat history.

**Friction 3: Didn't ask about the reference images upfront.** I suggested removing the city reference image at the end, but should have asked at the start: "what references are you attaching?" Since visual refs overpower text in Seedance, knowing what's attached is as important as the prompt itself.

## Lessons Learned

- **Negotiation: structural constraints > personal reluctance** — "the committee decides" is stronger than "we don't want to pay that much"
- **Seedance: visual refs overpower text** — if you attach a burning city image, Seedance will show a burning city regardless of text instructions → remove unwanted ref images
- **Prompt revision: ask what's working first** — before rewriting, confirm which parts to keep ("แอคชั่นโอเคอยู่แล้ว")
- **Surgical additions > full rewrites** — adding 2 targeted sentences to an existing prompt is better than rewriting the whole thing
- **Register switching is a real skill** — formal English email → spoken Thai summary → English creative prompt, all in one session
