## Lessons Learned

- In negotiation emails: **never show eagerness or commitment** — ask questions, gather info, let the other side reveal their floor
- When email threads cross departments, **always re-introduce context** — don't assume they read previous messages
- DewS prefers **text-based negotiation** over calls — enables WEnDyS assistance + creates paper trail
- Separate "asking for information" from "making an offer" — they are fundamentally different negotiation moves
- The Thai summary for พี่บอย shows the value of **register-switching** — formal English ↔ spoken Thai
