# Negotiation: Non-Committal Price Probing

**Date**: 2026-04-24
**Source**: rrr: wendys-oracle
**Context**: Tuya IoT Core pricing negotiation round 2

## Pattern

When negotiating with vendors on behalf of DewS:

1. **Default stance = non-committal** — never write "ready to proceed", "can commit", "willing to pay"
2. **Separate info-gathering from offer-making** — "what's your floor?" is different from "here's our counter"
3. **Let them reveal their floor first** — don't show our hand with specific numbers until we know their range
4. **When threads cross departments** — always re-introduce project context, don't assume continuity
5. **DewS prefers text over calls** — enables WEnDyS to help draft + creates paper trail as evidence
6. **Grant proposal constraint** — university projects can't just "pay", they need formal budget approval first

## Anti-Pattern (things I did wrong)

- Writing "ready to proceed within weeks" — too eager
- Proposing counter-offer numbers before knowing their true minimum
- Not noticing when a different department/person entered the thread
