# Vendor Negotiation Email Strategy

**Date**: 2026-04-23
**Source**: rrr: wendys-oracle
**Context**: Drafting Tuya IoT pricing negotiation email for EcoStove

## Pattern

When negotiating with tech vendors for academic/research projects:

1. **Never ask for free outright** — "academic/special pricing" is stronger than "free tier please"
2. **Lead with scale-up potential** — vendors invest in growth pipelines (12 → 300 sensors)
3. **Offer academic credibility** — published papers, conference mentions, government reports = free PR for vendor
4. **Use personal email for initial contact** — keeps project account clean, gives negotiation flexibility
5. **Don't reveal budget first** — let vendor propose, then negotiate down
6. **Ask about budget/strategy before drafting** — saves iteration rounds on tone calibration
