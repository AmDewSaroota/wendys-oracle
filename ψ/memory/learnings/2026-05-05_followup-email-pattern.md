# Follow-up Email Pattern

**Date**: 2026-05-05
**Source**: Tuya pricing follow-up
**Context**: Academic/research project needing vendor response

## Pattern

When writing follow-up emails to vendors/teams who haven't responded:

1. **Restate specific questions** — don't just say "following up on my previous email"
2. **Give them an easy out** — "even a brief response" lowers the barrier to reply
3. **Add urgency without pressure** — mention the grant/deadline context naturally
4. **Offer alternative routing** — "let me know if there's a better contact" gives them permission to redirect

## Anti-pattern

- Vague "just checking in" with no specifics
- Demanding tone
- Repeating the entire original email verbatim
