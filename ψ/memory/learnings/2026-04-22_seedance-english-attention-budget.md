# Seedance English Prompts — Attention Budget Rule

**Date**: 2026-04-22
**Source**: rrr: wendys-oracle
**Tags**: seedance, prompt-engineering, english, dreamina, attention-budget

## Pattern

Seedance has a limited **attention budget** — adding constraints in one area reduces intensity in another.

## Problem

Adding "faces the camera at all times" in 3 separate locations made the wing spread slow and smooth (lost explosive power), even though the same intensity words were present.

## Solution

**Pack constraint + action in the same sentence**, not separate paragraphs:

```
❌ The dragon faces the camera at all times.
   ...
   (later) both wings violently burst open

✅ chest still facing camera — then both wings VIOLENTLY BURST OPEN
```

## Bonus: English Works

- English prompts on Dreamina (Seedance 2.0) produce equivalent results to Chinese
- CAPS for power words helps: VIOLENTLY BURST OPEN, SLAM, TORN APART, BLASTS
- Equivalent to Chinese extreme: 猛然炸开, 急停顿挫, 空气被撕裂
